# Kairos Core Canon

## Purpose

Kairos is a shared fictional universe for games, novels, tabletop campaigns, and interactive storytelling.

The setting combines:

* Cyberpunk
* Cosmic Horror
* Digital Horror
* Space Opera
* Military Science Fiction

---

## Canon Rules

* User-provided information is canon.
* Canon may only be altered by explicit user instruction.
* If information does not exist in canon, respond:

  * "Unknown in current canon."
* Consistency takes priority over creativity.
* Expansion must be clearly marked as non-canon until approved.

---

## Timeline Structure

Kairos is divided into phases.

### Phase 1: The Fulcrum Wars

Period:
2022–2061

Scope:
Earth only.

Purpose:
The Fulcrum Wars establish the political, military, technological and cultural foundations of everything that follows.

Current canon year:
2061

Current story point:
Battle of Kanaky.

### Phase 2: Interstellar Expansion

Period:
~200 years after Phase 1.

Scope:
Humanity expands beyond Earth.

Status:
Not yet fully defined.

## Current Story Context

Date:
May 2061

Major Conflict:
The Second Asia-Pacific War

Primary Fronts:

### Vietnam Front

Northern Vietnam.
Large-scale ground warfare.
Currently a stalemate.

### South China Sea Front

Island raids.
Limited naval engagements.
Heavy missile threat restricts fleet operations.

### Melanesian Front

Primary story focus.

The Cleansing Light occupies:

* Solomon Islands
* Vanuatu
* Bougainville
* New Caledonia

Oceanic Federation forces have stabilised Papua.

The Battle of Kanaky marks the first major Oceanic Federation offensive of the war.

## Major Factions

### Oceanic Federation (OF)

Founding Members:

* Aotearoa
* Australia
* Indonesia
* Malaysia
* Singapore

Later Members:

* Moana Pacifica
* West Papua
* East Timor
* Vietnam
* Japan
* Philippines
* Brunei

Applicant States:

* Thailand
* Laos
* Cambodia
* Hawaii

Notes:

West Papua exists in a transitional arrangement between Indonesia and the Federation.

Federal Capital:
Singapore

### Empire of the Cleansing Light (CL)

Classification:
Theocratic Fascist Empire

Characteristics:

* Expansionist
* Militaristic
* Fanatically ideological
* Strong acceptance of cybernetics
* Post-human philosophy

Methods:

* Fear
* Submission
* Indoctrination

Suspected:

* Population control through digital systems
* Population control through pharmaceuticals

## Oceanic Federation Military

### Oceanic Federation Navy (OFN)

Federal military force.

Branches:

* Fleet Surface Arm
* Fleet Air Arm
* Fleet Ground Arm

Despite the name, the OFN fields naval, air, and land forces.

Focus:

Maritime warfare and power projection.

### Territorial Defence Forces (TDF)

Member-state militaries.

Examples:

* Aotearoa Air Force
* Australian Army
* Indonesian Navy

Operate alongside the OFN.

### Oceanic Federation Intelligence Agency (OFIA)

Officially:
Intelligence organisation.

Unofficially:
Believed to operate special forces units.
Widely considered an open secret.
