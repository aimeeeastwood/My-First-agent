These theatres are not symbolic — they define real military logistics, supply chains, and strategic priorities.

📍 CORE ZONE

Adjacent contested inland regions
⚔ STATUS

High-intensity sustained ground combat
Entrenched stalemate conditions
🌊 SOUTH CHINA SEA SUB-THEATRE
CHARACTERISTICS:

Sporadic naval and air engagements
Missile threat saturation from shore-based systems

Large fleet actions are heavily constrained due to anti-ship missile denial zones.
