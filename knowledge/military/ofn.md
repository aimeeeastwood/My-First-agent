# Oceanic Federation Navy

## Classification

Federal Military Service

## Abbreviation

OFN

## Parent Organisation

Oceanic Federation

## Founded

2044

---

## Overview

The Oceanic Federation Navy (OFN) is the federal military force of the Oceanic Federation.

Despite its name, the OFN is not solely a naval organisation. It operates across maritime, aerial and ground domains through a unified command structure designed around the defence of the Federation's maritime environment.

The OFN serves as the primary military force of the Federation and is responsible for expeditionary warfare, power projection, strategic deterrence, coalition operations and the defence of federal territory.

By 2061 the OFN is engaged in active combat against the Empire of the Cleansing Light throughout Southeast Asia and Melanesia.

---

## Structure

The OFN is divided into three primary operational branches.

### Fleet Surface Arm

Responsible for:

* Surface combatants
* Aircraft carriers
* Amphibious operations
* Logistics fleets
* Strategic sealift
* Maritime security

The Fleet Surface Arm forms the backbone of OFN power projection.

---

### Fleet Air Arm

Responsible for:

* Carrier aviation
* Air superiority
* Maritime strike operations
* Reconnaissance
* Electronic warfare
* Unmanned aerial systems

The Fleet Air Arm operates both carrier-based and land-based aircraft.

---

### Fleet Ground Arm

Responsible for:

* Marines
* Amphibious assault forces
* Expeditionary infantry
* Ground combat operations
* Occupation and stabilisation missions

The Fleet Ground Arm serves as the OFN's primary land warfare capability.

---

## Relationship with Territorial Defence Forces

The OFN does not replace the military forces of member states.

Individual nations maintain their own Territorial Defence Forces (TDF), including their armies, navies and air forces.

Examples include:

* Australian Army
* Indonesian Navy
* Aotearoa/New Zealand Air Force

The OFN operates alongside these organisations and serves as the Federation's unified federal military.

---

## Doctrine

The OFN was designed around the unique geography of the Federation.

Core doctrine emphasises:

* Maritime control
* Expeditionary operations
* Coalition warfare
* Long-range logistics
* Carrier-based air power
* Amphibious warfare

Because Federation territory spans thousands of islands and vast stretches of ocean, the OFN places exceptional importance on mobility and sea control.

## Current War

The OFN entered direct war against the Empire of the Cleansing Light following the invasion of Japan and the Federation's declaration of war in 2060.

As of May 2061, OFN forces are engaged across multiple theatres.

### Vietnam Front

Support operations against CL advances north of Hanoi.

### South China Sea Front

Island raids, air combat and naval skirmishes.

### Melanesian Front

Major campaign to reclaim Federation approaches and restore control of critical sea lanes.

The Battle of Kanaky represents the first major OFN offensive operation of the war.

---

## Reputation

The OFN is widely regarded as one of the world's most capable maritime military forces.

While smaller than many historical great-power militaries, it compensates through:

* Advanced technology
* Strong coalition integration
* High professional standards
* Flexible command structures
* Extensive carrier aviation capability

Its greatest strength lies in its ability to rapidly project force across the Pacific and Southeast Asia.
