# OFS Wellington (CVA-3)

## Canon Status
Active Service Vessel (2061 — Battle of Kanaky period)
Fast Attack Carrier (Canberra-class)
Third and final hull of Canberra-class production line

---

## Registry Designation
- **CVA-3 — OFS Wellington**
- Canberra-class Fast Attack Carrier (FAC)
- Final operational unit of Canberra-class doctrine

---

## Class Context

The Canberra-class represents the Oceanic Federation Navy’s first Fast Attack Carrier doctrine:
- distributed maritime warfare theory
- high-tempo strike operations
- mid-size carrier balance between presence and agility

Wellington is the **final iteration of this class**, incorporating all operational refinements developed during earlier Canberra-class deployments.

---

## Operational Role (2061)

At the onset of the Battle of Kanaky, OFS Wellington is an active frontline carrier deployed in Melanesian theatre operations.

Primary responsibilities include:
- forward air-maritime strike coordination
- support of OFN task group operations
- sea lane security across vulnerable Federation logistics corridors
- rapid response engagement in island-chain warfare environments

---

## Doctrinal Position

Wellington operates at the intersection of two evolving OFN doctrines:

### 1. Canberra-class doctrine (legacy)
- Fast Attack Carrier theory
- medium-sized carrier deployment groups
- emphasis on distributed engagement

### 2. Emerging wartime doctrine (Kuala Lumpur-class era)
- stealth-first carrier design
- survivability over presence
- reduced visibility operational strategy

Wellington is therefore considered:

> the final operational expression of pre-Kanaky carrier doctrine

---

## Strategic Importance in Battle of Kanaky

The Battle of Kanaky represents the first major conflict in which:
- Canberra-class carriers are fully committed
- Kuala Lumpur-class carriers are still under construction
- OFN doctrine is actively under revision during combat operations

Within this environment, Wellington functions as:
- a frontline strike carrier
- a command-support vessel in contested Melanesian waters
- a stabilising asset within rapidly evolving fleet doctrine

---

## Historical Note (Non-Canonical at Time of Service)

At the time of the Battle of Kanaky, Wellington is not considered a legend or symbolic vessel.

Her historical significance:
- is not yet established
- will be determined retrospectively after the war
- becomes prominent only through later historiography of the Second Asia-Pacific War

## Cultural Status (2061)

OFS Wellington (CVA-3) is the final Canberra-class Fast Attack Carrier and serves as a frontline asset during the Battle of Kanaky.
