# 🤖 UA-55 Will’O’Wisp — Autonomous Loyal Wingman System

## 🧭 Overview

The UA-55 Will’O’Wisp is an unmanned autonomous combat drone developed as part of the 6FAEX program’s distributed air combat architecture.

Unlike the FA-55 Banshee and EA-55 Siren, the UA-55 does not share a common airframe lineage and is significantly smaller in scale.

It is designed as a fully autonomous, network-integrated “loyal wingman” platform operating in close coordination with manned 6FAEX aircraft.

---

## ✈️ Physical Characteristics

- **Size Class:** roughly equivalent to WWII single-engine fighter aircraft  
- **Configuration:** tailless “tic-tac” fuselage with short wing structures  
- **Cockpit:** none (fully unmanned)  
- **Crew:** autonomous AI system with remote command integration  

---

## ⚙️ Design Role

The UA-55 is designed specifically for:

- distributed swarm operations  
- sensor expansion and battlefield mapping  
- decoy and electronic saturation roles  
- missile interception / attrition absorption  
- escort augmentation for FA-55 strike packages  

---

## 🎮 Control Integration (6FAEX Network)

The UA-55 is not operated as a standalone drone.

It is integrated into the 6FAEX command architecture:

### 🛩️ FA-55 Banshee Control Capacity
- can directly command up to **4 UA-55 units**
- used for localised tactical coordination in strike packages

### 📡 EA-55 Siren Control Capacity
- can command up to **10 UA-55 units**
- acts as system-level coordinator for swarm behaviour and electronic warfare integration

---

## 🧠 Operational Behaviour Model

The UA-55 functions as a:

- forward sensor extension layer  
- disposable combat presence multiplier  
- electronic warfare decoy platform  
- autonomous escort / interception screen  

It is designed to operate in environments where manned aircraft survivability is degraded.

---

## ⚓ Doctrinal Role within 6FAEX

Within the 6FAEX system hierarchy:

- EA-55 Siren → command, perception, electromagnetic control  
- FA-55 Banshee → kinetic execution platform  
- UA-55 Will’O’Wisp → distributed autonomous force multiplier  

---

## 📌 Summary

The UA-55 Will’O’Wisp is a small, unmanned, highly autonomous loyal wingman system designed to extend the reach, survivability, and battlefield density of FA-55 and EA-55 operations through scalable distributed control networks.
