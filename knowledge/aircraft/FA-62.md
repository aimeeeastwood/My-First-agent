# FA-62 Sprite Multirole Light Stealth Fighter

## Canon Status
Development / Early Introduction Phase (2062–2063 deployment window)

---

## Overview

The FA-62 Sprite is a low-cost, lightweight stealth multirole fighter developed as a complementary system to the FA-55 Banshee.

It is often informally referred to as:
- “Baby Banshee”
- “Budget Banshee”

These terms are unofficial and primarily used in operational or pilot culture contexts.

---

## Strategic Context

Initial OFN doctrine plans intended for FA-55 Banshee-class aircraft to be distributed across both:
- Oceanic Federation Navy (OFN)
- Territorial Defence Forces (TDF)

However, cost, maintenance complexity, and strategic allocation constraints led to a doctrinal split:

- FA-55 retained exclusively by OFN naval aviation forces
- TDF required a lower-cost, scalable alternative

This requirement led to the development of the FA-62 Sprite program.

---

## Design Philosophy

The FA-62 Sprite is designed around:

- reduced procurement and operational cost
- scalable stealth integration (not full FA-55 capability set)
- high sortie availability for distributed defence networks
- compatibility with TDF infrastructure and training pipelines

---

## Role in Doctrine

The Sprite functions as:

- TDF primary modern air combat platform
- companion aircraft to FA-55 carrier strike groups in joint operations
- high-density airspace patrol and interception platform

---

## Relationship to FA-55 Banshee

The FA-62 does NOT replace the FA-55.

Instead, it forms a paired system:

- FA-55: strategic stealth carrier strike dominance
- FA-62: distributed tactical air control and support layer

Together they form a complementary air combat architecture.

---

## Summary

The FA-62 Sprite represents the democratization of advanced stealth-era air combat capability for non-naval forces within the Oceanic Federation system.

## ✈️ FA-62 Sprite — Variant Doctrine

The FA-62 Sprite is produced in two primary operational configurations:

---

## 🚢 Carrier Variant (FA-62C)

Designed for integration with Oceanic Federation Navy carrier air wings.

### Characteristics:
- Catapult / STOBAR compatible (depending on carrier configuration)
- Optimised for strike group coordination
- Operates alongside FA-55 Banshee in layered carrier doctrine

### Role:
- fleet air defence augmentation
- secondary strike support
- distributed stealth screening operations

---

## 🛫 VTOL Variant (FA-62V)

Designed for Territorial Defence Forces (TDF), particularly Australia and Aotearoa.

### Characteristics:
- vertical / short takeoff capability
- dispersed base operations (island, coastal, austere environments)
- rapid redeployment between low-infrastructure airfields

### Role:
- homeland air defence
- rapid interception response
- distributed airspace denial networks

---

## 🧭 Doctrinal Note

The FA-62 program does not include a conventional runway-only variant.

This reflects a core Oceanic Federation doctrine principle:

> air power is inherently maritime in structure, even when land-based
