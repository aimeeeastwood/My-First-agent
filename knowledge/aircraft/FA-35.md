✈️ FA-35 Family (2061 context)

The FA-35 is now a mature legacy multirole platform lineage, comparable to long-serving WWII-era airframes that remained relevant through continuous upgrades, but gradually lost doctrinal dominance.

It functions as:

secondary air superiority platform
strike support aircraft
mass-availability fleet fighter
coalition / allied interoperability airframe

But it is no longer the doctrinal “top tier” system.

## ✈️ FA-35 Lightning II — Legacy Multirole Fighter Family (2061 Status)

By 2061, the FA-35 platform family is a long-service multirole airframe lineage with multiple advanced subvariants in active operation.

Common operational variants include:
- FA-35J
- FA-35OA
- FA-35ON

These represent iterative improvements in avionics, survivability systems, and network integration.

---

## 🧭 Historical Role Context

The FA-35 was originally a 5th-generation multirole aircraft designed for flexible air dominance and strike capability.

By 2061, it occupies a legacy-but-still-active role in global air forces, particularly in:
- coalition operations
- secondary theatre deployments
- force supplementation roles alongside more advanced platforms

---

## 📉 Operational Limitations (Relative to FA-55 / FI-45 class systems)

Despite upgrades, FA-35 variants continue to exhibit structural limitations compared to 6th-generation or specialised interceptor platforms:

- reduced climb performance relative to dedicated interceptors (FI-45 class)
- limited operational range compared to carrier strike platforms (FA-55 class)
- constrained payload capacity in high-threat environments
- reduced survivability in dense modern air-defence systems

---

## 🧭 Doctrinal Status

The FA-35 family is best understood as:

> a mature, heavily upgraded legacy multirole system that remains operationally useful but no longer defines front-line air combat doctrine

It is analogous in role continuity to long-serving combat aircraft families from earlier historical eras, where incremental upgrades extend service life beyond original design expectations.

---

## 📌 Summary

The FA-35 is not obsolete in function, but it is no longer a leading-edge platform in 2061 doctrine.

It persists due to:
- global fleet size
- upgrade extensibility
- logistical and interoperability value

rather than doctrinal superiority.
