## 🧠 Aircraft Lineage Principle (Kairos Canon Rule)

In Kairos aerospace development, chronological order does not determine design lineage.

Aircraft may be:
- developed after one another chronologically
- influenced by shared engineering knowledge
- informed by operational lessons from earlier platforms

However:

> later aircraft are not inherently “upgrades” or “descendants” of earlier aircraft.

---

## ✈️ FI-45 → FA-55 Relationship Clarification

The FI-45 Raiden predates the FA-55 Banshee in operational deployment history.

However, the FA-55 is not a “new Raiden” or successor model.

Instead:

- FI-45 is a national interceptor platform designed for homeland air defence
- FA-55 is a purpose-built carrier stealth strike fighter developed from broader multinational design lessons, including those learned from FI-45 operations

The relationship is therefore:

> experiential influence, not structural evolution
