# 📡 EA-55 Siren — Electronic Warfare Variant of the FA-55 Platform

## 🧭 Overview

The EA-55 Siren is an electronic warfare (EW) variant of the FA-55 Banshee carrier stealth strike fighter, designed for electronic attack, battlefield hacking, sensor disruption, and network warfare operations within contested maritime theatres.

It shares the same core airframe and propulsion system as the FA-55, including the Holton Bronco variable bypass turbofan.

---

## ✈️ Airframe Commonality

The EA-55 is structurally derived from the FA-55 platform:

- identical fuselage and stealth shaping architecture  
- identical carrier landing and launch systems  
- identical Holton Bronco engine integration  
- shared maintenance and logistics pipeline with FA-55 units  

This ensures full interoperability within carrier air wings.

---

## 👥 Crew Configuration

Unlike the single-seat FA-55 Banshee, the EA-55 features a dual-crew cockpit:

- **Pilot:** flight control, navigation, carrier operations  
- **EWO (“Hacker”):** electronic warfare officer responsible for:
  - network intrusion operations  
  - sensor jamming and deception  
  - battlefield signal manipulation  
  - coordination of drone / unmanned systems  

The EWO role is central to EA-55 operational identity.

---

## 🔧 System Modifications

To accommodate its electronic warfare focus, the EA-55 replaces or modifies several FA-55 systems:

### Removed / Reduced:
- internal cannon system  
- portions of kinetic strike weapon hardpoints  

### Added / Enhanced:
- advanced EW pod integration bays  
- extended power generation for electronic systems  
- reinforced cooling systems for sustained electronic operations  
- expanded sensor and signal intelligence arrays  

---

## ⚡ Propulsion System

The EA-55 uses the same Holton Bronco Variable Bypass Turbofan as the FA-55 Banshee, enabling:

- high endurance loiter over electromagnetic battlespace zones  
- stable power delivery for sustained EW operations  
- carrier-compatible thrust profile identical to FA-55 fleet requirements  

---

## 🎯 Operational Role

The EA-55 Siren functions as:

- electronic warfare escort for FA-55 strike packages  
- electromagnetic spectrum dominance platform  
- sensor suppression and deception node  
- coordination hub for unmanned systems (UA-55 Will’O’Wisp integration)  

---

## ⚓ Doctrine Integration

EA-55 units are typically deployed alongside FA-55 Banshees within carrier strike groups.

Their primary function is to:

> shape and degrade the battlespace environment before and during FA-55 strike execution

This creates a layered air combat system:
- EA-55: controls the information battlefield  
- FA-55: executes kinetic strikes within that controlled space  

---

## 📌 Summary

The EA-55 Siren is not a separate aircraft family, but a mission-adapted variant of the FA-55 Banshee platform.

It trades kinetic weapon capacity for electronic warfare dominance, enabling the Oceanic Federation Navy to conduct integrated kinetic–electromagnetic operations from a unified carrier airframe ecosystem.
