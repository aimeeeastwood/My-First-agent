## 🧠 Aircraft Role Separation Principle

Aircraft in Kairos are not defined by generation alone, but by operational doctrine.

Different platforms may exist simultaneously because they fulfil fundamentally different battlefield roles.

---

## ✈️ FI-45 vs FA-55 vs FA-35

### FI-45 Raiden
- Dedicated homeland air defence interceptor
- Operates under TDF and national air defence structures
- Designed for rapid response within sovereign airspace

### FA-35 Lightning II (Legacy Multirole Class)
- General-purpose multirole fighter
- Represents transitional 5th-generation doctrine
- Used across multiple allied and legacy air forces

### FA-55 Banshee
- 6th-generation carrier-based stealth strike fighter
- Designed specifically for Oceanic Federation Navy doctrine
- Optimised for maritime expeditionary warfare

---

## 🧭 Replacement Logic

- FA-55 does NOT replace FI-45
  → because FI-45 is a homeland interceptor

- FA-55 replaces FA-35 in OFN operational doctrine
  → because FA-35 is general-purpose multirole aircraft

---

## 📌 Core Principle

> Aircraft replacement is determined by mission role compatibility, not technological progression or chronology.
