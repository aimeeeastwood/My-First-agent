# KAIROS CANON TIMELINE

## Phase 1: The Fulcrum Wars

### 2022–May 2061

---

# Era 1: Divergence and Early Crises (2022–2039)

### 2022

* Russia invades Ukraine.
* Global temperatures reach record highs.

### 2023

* Violence escalates across Gaza and the West Bank.
* Israel-Iran tensions continue rising.

### 2027

* Ukraine-Russia peace treaty signed.
* Borders largely revert to pre-2014 lines.
* Both nations emerge economically and demographically devastated.

### 2028

* Global recession begins.
* USD experiences severe collapse.
* US, EU, and China conduct emergency economic interventions.
* Natural disasters place additional strain on governments.
* Civil unrest rises worldwide.

### 2030

* China and the United States avoid military conflict over Taiwan due to economic limitations.

### 2031

* China's economy contracts for the first time in the modern era.

### 2032

* USD loses reserve currency status.
* Global depression deepens.
* Israel-Palestine violence intensifies.

### 2033

* A coup removes the Israeli government.
* A secular left-wing coalition takes power.
* Palestinians receive equal legal rights within the state.

### 2034

* Terrorist attack on the United States Congress kills or injures roughly half its members.
* Martial law declared.

### 2035–2036

* Climate disasters intensify.
* South Pacific nations begin regional cooperation discussions.
* These talks become the foundation of the future Oceanic Federation.

### 2038

* The United States fractures.

* Texas, California, Oregon, Washington and others secede.

* Successor states emerge including:

  * Southern League
  * Republic of Cascadia
  * Kingdom of Hawaii
  * Alaska
  * Rocky Free States
  * Eastern United States (EUS)

* The European Union begins fragmenting.

* Southern China demands reform.

* Beijing refuses.

* Second Chinese Civil War begins.

---

# Era 2: Climate, Independence and Regional Realignment (2040–2044)

### 2040

* Parts of Central America, Africa and Central Asia become periodically uninhabitable due to heat.
* Climate refugee crisis accelerates.

### 2040

* Australia, Aotearoa and Canada abolish their monarchies.
* All become republics.
* Aotearoa formally aligns its constitutional framework with the Treaty of Waitangi.

### 2042

* Southern China and Taiwan establish the Republic of China.
* Shanghai is abandoned during the conflict.
* Hong Kong becomes the southern capital.

### 2044

* Singapore Treaty signed.
* Oceanic Federation officially founded.

Founding Members:

* Aotearoa

* Australia

* Indonesia

* Malaysia

* Singapore

* Construction begins on federal military infrastructure.

* Oceanic Federation Navy (OFN) established.

---

# Era 3: Rising Tensions and Early Conflicts (2054–2056)

### 2054

* Violence across western China reaches unprecedented levels.
* Global attention remains divided.

### 2054–2055

## Revelation of the Cleansing Light

* The Empire of the Cleansing Light (CL) emerges publicly.

* Western China and Siberia fall rapidly.

* The People's Republic of China collapses under cyberwarfare and multi-front attacks.

* North Korea falls shortly afterwards.

* South Korea and Japan begin emergency military preparations.

The Cleansing Light is revealed as:

* Highly technologically advanced.
* Theocratic.
* Expansionist.
* Ideologically uncompromising.

The CL identifies the Oceanic Federation as its primary geopolitical obstacle.

---

# Era 4: Full-Scale Conflict and Militarisation (2057–2059)

### 2057

#### Battle of Hong Kong

* Six-month urban campaign.
* One of the bloodiest battles of the war.

#### Oceanic Federation Militarisation

OFN expands dramatically.

Major programs include:

* Aircraft carriers
* Destroyers
* Frigates
* Submarines

Construction begins on:

* OFS Canberra

#### Japan Requests Assistance

Oceanic and Japanese engineers begin the 6FAEX program.

### 2057–2058

#### North American War

* Southern League attacks EUS agricultural regions.
* Conflict spreads across central North America.

#### Europe

* AMOC collapse confirmed.
* Severe cooling events begin.

### 2058

* China effectively falls.

* Korea falls.

* Japan stands alone.

* Philippines applies for Oceanic Federation membership.

### 2059

#### 6FAEX Program Completed

Aircraft enter production:

* FA-55 Banshee
* EA-55 Siren
* UA-55 Will'O'Wisp

#### OFS Canberra Commissioned

#### Vietnam Front Opens

* Cleansing Light invades Vietnam.
* Oceanic Federation, Cascadia and Canada provide assistance.
* Front stabilises.

The Vietnam Front becomes:

* Trench warfare
* Drone warfare
* Attritional stalemate

---

# Era 5: Oceanic Federation Enters the War (2060–May 2061)

### 2060

#### Federation Expansion

New Member States:

* Japan
* Philippines
* Vietnam
* Brunei
* East Timor
* West Papua
* Moana Pacifica

#### Japan Invaded

* Cleansing Light launches invasion.
* Tactical nuclear strike devastates Tokyo.
* Millions displaced.
* Japanese government relocates to Singapore.

#### Oceanic Federation Declares War

* OF formally enters the Second Asia-Pacific War.

### Early 2061

#### OF Carrier Fleet Expands

Operational Fleet Carriers:

* OFS Canberra
* OFS Jakarta
* OFS Wellington

#### Melanesian Front Opens

Following stalemate in Vietnam:

The Cleansing Light launches a surprise southern offensive.

Occupied Territories:

* Solomon Islands
* Vanuatu
* Bougainville
* New Caledonia

Papua:

* Local resistance and OF forces halt further advances.

### May 2061

# Battle of Kanaky Begins

* First major Oceanic Federation offensive of the war.
* Objective:

  * Liberate Kanaky/New Caledonia.
  * Secure sea lanes to Cascadia and Canada.
  * Halt CL expansion in Melanesia.

The battle marks the beginning of the primary Kairos narrative.

#### Reputation Earned

* OFS Wellington becomes known as:
  "The Scrappy Carrier That Could."
